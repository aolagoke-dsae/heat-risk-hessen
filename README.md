# Heat Risk Hessen

**Heat, deaths and who lives where it's hottest, 2000–2025** · German title:
*Hitzerisiko Hessen*

How has summer heat in Hessen changed since 2000, and where in its cities
does it concentrate? How many deaths does it cause, has the population grown
less sensitive to it, and who lives where the heat is greatest? Deaths, air
and surface temperatures and the population grid are observed data, and the
heat-death figures are checked first against the Robert Koch Institute's
published estimates.

**Author:** Dr. Adewole Olagoke · [aolagoke-dsae](https://github.com/aolagoke-dsae)
